 ### Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard

This Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard is designed to help businesses understand why customers leave and where the biggest risks lie. 

🔑 Key Metrics at the Top
Total Customers (7,043): The size of the customer base being analyzed.

Churn Rate (26.5%): The proportion of customers leaving — quite high, suggesting retention issues.

Monthly Revenue at Risk (139,131): The potential revenue loss if churned customers don’t return.

Avg Monthly Charges (64.76): Average bill per customer, useful for revenue forecasting.

📊 Visual Insights
Churn Rate by Contract Type

Month-to-month contracts show the highest churn (~40%).

One-year and two-year contracts have much lower churn, proving that longer commitments reduce customer exits.

Churn by Internet Service & Payment Method

Fiber optic customers churn the most (~40%), possibly due to service issues or competition.

DSL and “No internet service” customers churn less.

Monthly Charges vs Tenure Scatter Plot

Shows how customer longevity (tenure) relates to monthly charges.

Useful for spotting whether high-paying customers stay longer or leave sooner.

Churn Rate by Tenure Bucket

New customers (0–12 months) churn the most.

The longer a customer stays, the less likely they are to leave — loyalty builds over time.

📋 Churn Detail Table
Lists individual customers with contract type, charges, and tenure.

Example: Customers on two-year contracts with high tenure (68–72 months) have 0% churn, showing strong retention.

🎯 What This Dashboard Tells You
Retention Strategy: Focus on reducing churn among month-to-month and fiber optic customers.

Customer Lifecycle: Early-stage customers are most vulnerable — onboarding and engagement programs could help.

Revenue Protection: High-value customers on long-term contracts are stable, so the risk lies mainly in short-term, high-churn groups.
